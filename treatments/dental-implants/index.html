Dental Implants / A permanent fix for missing teeth

A dental implant is a small titanium post placed into the jawbone, where it fuses with the bone and acts like a natural tooth root. It then supports a crown, a bridge or a denture.

Unlike removable dentures, implants don't slip or need adhesives. They help preserve the jawbone and facial structure, so your smile and your face keep their natural shape.

  • Permanent solution for missing teeth
  • Restores natural function and appearance
  • Helps preserve jawbone and facial structure
  • Improves speech and everyday comfort

Is it right for you? / Signs you may need this

Not sure? Book a consultation and we'll examine your teeth and explain every option honestly, with no pressure to go ahead.

A missing tooth

One or more gaps from an extraction, injury or a failed old filling.

Loose dentures

Dentures that slip, click or make eating and speaking difficult.

Difficulty chewing

Avoiding certain foods because of gaps at the back of the mouth.

Healthy gums & bone

Good gum health and enough bone, which we check on digital X-rays.
